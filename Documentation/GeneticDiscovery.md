# Genetic strategy discovery for LEAN

This branch adds an independent, deterministic genetic search module and connects it to LEAN's existing optimizer and backtest runner. The Algory investigation remains behavioral research only; no Algory executable, service, protected code, or license mechanism is used here.

The repository baseline is upstream LEAN commit `22404ea28b8d3f3c9eb74d6ed9f0444426f8e417` (upstream `master` at the fork point, rechecked on 2026-10-27). The exact commit plus the SDK pin makes the build baseline reproducible.

## Architecture

- `StrategyDiscovery/QuantConnect.Genetic.csproj` is the standalone engine. A genome selects long-only trend-following or mean-reversion logic, a primary minute/hour/daily resolution with an optional slower timeframe confirmation, moving-average and volatility periods, entry/exit thresholds, position count, and gross exposure. Higher-timeframe filters are built from LEAN consolidators over the primary subscription, and an active filter must be ready and directionally agree before the primary signal can create a portfolio target.
- `Optimizer/Strategies/GeneticSearchOptimizationStrategy.cs` implements LEAN's `IOptimizationStrategy`. It submits candidate parameter sets to LEAN; `ConsoleLeanOptimizer` starts independent LEAN processes and bounds concurrency. Growth and Consistency populations evolve separately. Random candidates provide a baseline.
- Universe selection defaults to the fixed `discovery.symbols` list. Optional `discovery.universe-mode=TopDollarVolume` selects up to `discovery.universe-size` US equity securities daily from LEAN point-in-time coarse data, using a $5 minimum price and $25 million minimum dollar volume. Configured symbols remain as anchors and may include ETFs. Newly added securities receive indicator and return-history warmup; removed securities are liquidated before active strategy state is dropped. Optimizer and paper universe modes and sizes must match, and the supervisor checks this before starting.
- `Algorithm.CSharp/GeneticDiscoveryAlgorithm.cs` evaluates one genome using LEAN securities, indicators, portfolio targets, brokerage fees, and slippage. U.S. equity orders use `AlpacaEquityRegulatoryFeeModel` for estimated CAT fees on buys and sells plus SEC and FINRA TAF fees on sells. Its live-mode guard requires explicit strategy opt-in, `alpaca-paper-trading=true`, and the `AlpacaBrokerage` adapter; other live brokerage adapters are rejected. It budgets remaining position slots and gross exposure after existing holdings outside the managed symbol set, and emits zero targets only for its managed symbols when signals disappear or shutdown is requested. Portfolio correlation uses exact shared return intervals; a candidate is excluded while a pair has fewer than 20 aligned samples. Intraday equity target changes are deferred until a regular-session bar so LEAN does not turn an after-hours market order into a brokerage-invalid market-on-open order. `StrategyDiscovery/PortfolioDrawdownGuard.cs` persists the account high watermark and a latched drawdown trip across LEAN process restarts; the paper supervisor stops optimization/restarts after a trip. A file-based emergency stop is checked on data events. Brokerage disconnects pause new target submissions and liquidation orders until reconnection; an invalid order latches the execution safety gate, with liquidation deferred to the next data event to avoid reentrant order submission, and stops the algorithm when flat. Simulated market orders add a configurable half-spread floor (default 5 basis points full spread) plus LEAN's volume-share impact model; the spread floor is an assumption, not quote-derived execution data. The equity regulatory fee estimate uses Alpaca's fee schedule revised 2026-09-17.
- Training generations are scored on the configured training dates. Finalists from both evolved populations and random candidates are then rerun across configurable, contiguous chronological windows in the disjoint validation date interval. Evolved finalists also get deterministic one-axis parameter probes around their fast/slow periods, entry/exit thresholds, and available slower confirmation timeframes. A finalist must finish every central window, satisfy constraints in every central window, and have valid results for every requested probe. Its score is the lower of central lower-median fitness and neighbor lower-median fitness. JSONL evaluation history and ranked training/validation records are written under LEAN's results folder. The ranking file compares evolved finalists with the median held-out random baseline and records probe count, valid probe count, weakest neighbor fitness, and fitness delta. When LEAN provides closed trades, history also includes a seeded IID bootstrap summary of trade-level returns.
- The example algorithm runs scheduled health reviews every configured number of calendar days, emits a reoptimization request in its log, and retires after repeated negative review periods or a configured drawdown. A separate supervisor still needs to consume that request, launch a new optimizer run, and restart the algorithm with an approved genome.

The engine's seeded generator makes candidate creation and evolution reproducible. Evaluation order does not affect the next generation because breeding begins only after every candidate in that generation has completed. LEAN's optimizer controls process concurrency. Randomized-baseline comparisons report the empirical tail probability and its Bonferroni adjustment across evolved validation finalists. This controls finalist comparison multiplicity only; it does not correct the training-stage search or the fact that randomized baselines are selected by training fitness. Treat the values as diagnostics, not confirmatory evidence. The default example validates two random baselines, too few to resolve a 5% tail threshold; increase `validation-baseline-count` and `random-baseline-size` for finer resolution. Paper promotion requires at least one held-out random baseline and rejects evolved candidates whose robustness-adjusted fitness does not exceed the baseline median.

## Build and run a local discovery search

The SDK is pinned in `global.json` to .NET SDK 10.0.403. Build from the repository root, then launch the optimizer with the checked-in config:

```powershell
dotnet build QuantConnect.Lean.sln
Push-Location Optimizer.Launcher/bin/Debug
dotnet QuantConnect.Optimizer.Launcher.dll --config ../../../examples/genetic-discovery/optimizer.json
Pop-Location
```

`optimizer.json` is a modest search example with three chronological validation windows and four parameter probes per evolved finalist. `optimizer-smoke.json` runs ten LEAN backtests on the March 2021 SPY data archive, with one validation window and one probe per evolved finalist. Ensure LEAN's matching US equity data is installed before running. Result and LEAN launcher paths are relative to `Optimizer.Launcher/bin/Debug`.

The current interface accepts a non-empty LEAN optimizer `parameters` list for compatibility, while the genetic strategy itself emits genome parameters. The `discovery.marker` static parameter is a required placeholder and is ignored by the algorithm.

To use point-in-time coarse-universe selection, set `universe-mode` to `TopDollarVolume` and `universe-size` to the same value in optimizer settings and paper overlay parameters (`discovery.universe-mode` and `discovery.universe-size`). The checked-in examples default to `Configured`. The local integration fixture uses the 2014 coarse archive; local coarse coverage is sparse, and availability of coarse selection in an Alpaca live-data session has not been verified.

## Look-ahead prefix-invariance check

Run `examples/genetic-discovery/verify-prefix-invariance.ps1` from PowerShell after building the launcher and algorithm. It launches the same fixed SPY genome twice through LEAN's local data reader, once ending 2021-02-15 and once ending 2021-03-31, then compares filled orders through 2021-02-10 and validates each run's persisted drawdown state. The script requires at least one fill and saves complete runs and launcher logs under `%TEMP%\lean-prefix-invariance` by default. The latest run completed all three prefix checks: 146 daily fills, 67 hourly/daily-confirmation fills, and 355 multi-asset fills through the cutoff matched their longer-history runs. The multi-asset test runs SPY, QQQ, and IWM together; LEAN filled all three symbols, and each logged portfolio selection stayed within three positions and 60% gross target exposure. A same-genome LEAN cost-stress comparison on SPY reported 9.939% net profit with a 5 bps full-spread floor and 2.628% with a 200 bps floor. The 5 bps run recorded $7.17 of Alpaca equity regulatory fees. A separate 2019–2020 SPY run tripped the 5% account drawdown guard, persisted the latch, liquidated to flat, and submitted no later strategy orders. This confirms spread, volume-impact slippage, the equity fee estimate, and account drawdown shutdown affect simulated results. This cost sensitivity check does not calibrate the assumption to historical quotes. The dynamic-universe prefix check matched 6 fills through 2014-04-02 between short and longer histories; the full run selected changing securities from dated coarse records, including AAPL, and filled IWM alongside the SPY anchor. These are causal prefix checks for this algorithm/data path and bounded portfolio integration checks; they do not prove general absence of leakage.

## Alpaca paper execution

`StrategyDiscovery.Supervisor` runs the optimizer on a schedule, requires a fresh ranking file, approves only evolved candidates that pass per-window trade/drawdown gates and probe checks, and starts LEAN using the promoted genome. The supervisor keeps the current paper process running during optimization. For promotion or shutdown it creates the algorithm's emergency-stop file, waits for managed positions to close and LEAN to exit, then starts the replacement. Optimizer failures leave a running paper process untouched. Unexpected paper-process exits use exponential backoff and stop after the configured consecutive-failure limit. Its JSONL audit and child process logs go under the configured output directory.

The supervisor is Alpaca-paper-only. It refuses to start without both the `--enable-paper` command-line switch and `ALGORY_ALPACA_API_KEY` / `ALGORY_ALPACA_API_SECRET` environment variables. It merges `examples/genetic-discovery/paper-config.overlay.json` over LEAN's base config, forces `environment=live-alpaca`, `alpaca-paper-trading=true`, and the algorithm paper gate, and rejects optimizer/paper mismatches in symbols or risk caps. A single-instance file lock prevents two supervisors using the same output directory. The supervisor records the LEAN child PID and start time in `active-paper-session.json`; after a supervisor crash it fails closed if that same process is still alive, so an operator must inspect or stop it before relaunching. Dead or reused PIDs clear the stale marker. The persistent portfolio drawdown state is stored as `portfolio-drawdown-state.json` beside the emergency-stop file; once tripped, automatic optimization and paper restarts stop until an operator reviews and resets that state. The generated config with credentials is ephemeral under the current user's temporary directory and is deleted after the LEAN child exits normally; a supervisor crash can leave it behind for manual cleanup. The supervisor has no real-money mode.

To build and run the unattended paper supervisor after setting Alpaca **paper** credentials through a secret manager:

```powershell
dotnet build QuantConnect.Lean.sln
dotnet StrategyDiscovery.Supervisor/bin/Debug/QuantConnect.Genetic.Supervisor.dll --config examples/genetic-discovery/supervisor.json --enable-paper
```

`Ctrl+C` requests shutdown through the emergency-stop file. A manually present emergency-stop file is honored and prevents a restart. Running this workflow has not been demonstrated because this environment has no Alpaca paper credentials. Real-money live trading remains disabled by default and is not supported by this supervisor.

### Local package inspection (REA, static)

On 2026-10-27, REA inspected the installed Algory 1.5.3.1.8 package without executing it. The application has separate `Algory.exe`, `AlgoryHUD.exe`, and `Engine.exe` files. Static archive inspection identified both `Algory.exe` and `Engine.exe` as PyInstaller one-file packages carrying CPython 3.13. `Engine.exe` is the likely strategy-engine host: its archive contains an `Algory_Engine_v129` entry, a `PYZ.pyz/Algory_Genes_v16.pyc` module, and a compiled `MetaTrader5` package. The smaller `Algory.exe` package instead contains an `Algory_Dashboard_v119` entry plus `Algory_Genes_v16.pyc`, `Algory_UI_Lib_v1.pyc`, and `Algory_Vault_v1.pyc`. REA verified the inspected archive entries against their container integrity metadata.

This narrows the engine location to the Python payload hosted by `Engine.exe`, with related strategy code also bundled in the dashboard package. It does not yet prove which bytecode functions perform breeding, fitness scoring, or backtesting, or whether the GUI launches this engine for a particular workflow. No Algory or engine process was running during this inspection; only the HUD server/window processes were present. The currently available static evidence therefore does not establish runtime call paths, cloud responsibilities, paid entitlements, or function-level implementation. The previous controlled runtime observation recorded MT5 initialization attempts and historical-data requests to an Algory service, but those observations do not prove where all strategy calculations or persistence occur. The installed package also contains a `pyarmor_runtime_011532` support directory; protected modules were not decrypted or bypassed. No third-party binary or code is used by this implementation.

The identified engine host and embedded strategy module are stronger evidence than the prior shell-level identification, but exact Algory genome, genetic operators, fitness formulas, and backtest implementation remain unverified. This project reimplements those behaviors independently and does not evade protections.

The observed application boundary is partial: the desktop UI/HUD and protected engine process run locally; the runtime attempted MetaTrader 5 initialization and fetched historical data from an Algory service. Available evidence does not establish which ranking, storage, portfolio, or monitoring features are local versus server-side, nor which exact capabilities are subscription entitlements. No license bypass is attempted.

## Limitations and remaining work

- Optimizer and LEAN paper processes are coordinated by the supervisor. A process identity marker and single-instance lock prevent duplicate paper sessions after a supervisor crash, but they do not adopt the orphaned process or restore brokerage state; the operator must inspect and reconcile it. A process-boundary recovery test starts a child process, exits its launcher, and verifies a restarted supervisor detects and leaves the orphan untouched.
- Walk-forward validation partitions the holdout into contiguous calendar windows and scores the lower median fitness. It does not add purging/embargo gaps or carry open positions between independent LEAN backtests. Parameter probes alter one genome value at a time by fixed increments; this local sensitivity check does not establish broad parameter stability. Monte Carlo diagnostics use closed-trade P&L divided by entry notional and IID bootstrap resampling; randomized significance tests are not implemented.
- Multi-timeframe confirmation is a slower directional/regime filter using the same periods and thresholds as the primary signal; it does not evolve independent parameters per timeframe or support a faster confirmation series. The local integration check exercised hourly SPY bars with daily confirmation indicators through readiness and checked that no invalid order tripped the safety gate.
- `TopDollarVolume` uses LEAN's dated coarse records rather than a present-day survivor list, but ranks by previous-day price and dollar volume only; no fundamental quality filters are applied. Local coarse records cover only a sparse 2014 sample, and live coarse selection availability with Alpaca has not been verified. The available SPY daily archive ends on 2021-03-31, so other smoke runs cannot test current market regimes or long-span corporate-action behavior. Alpaca regulatory rates are fixed to its 2026-09-17 schedule for every historical date; LEAN charges them per order without Alpaca's daily account-level per-fee rounding, and this model uses the NMS per-share CAT rate rather than the OTC executed-share adjustment, so this is an approximate cost model, not a historical fee ledger. The integration smoke script runs AAPL across its August 2020 split and dividend in adjusted mode, then runs a raw-mode buy-and-hold check that asserts one share becomes four and a positive dividend is credited to the held position. Cash-in-lieu handling and broad corporate-action coverage remain unverified.
- Account slot/exposure calculations include outside-universe brokerage holdings, but cannot attribute positions in the configured symbols to other algorithms or manual orders. Concurrent algorithms can race these checks, so use a dedicated paper account until broker-level coordination is implemented and tested.
- LEAN Engine's brokerage message router is exercised with emitted `BacktestingBrokerage` disconnect/reconnect events, including the strategy order gate. Invalid-order shutdown has callback tests but no rejected-order event injection through the brokerage transaction pipeline. The supervisor recovery test exercises a surviving child across launcher exit, but it does not crash an actual `SupervisorHost` while it manages a LEAN process or restore brokerage state. Paper-session order outcomes and concurrent broker-level risk coordination still need integration tests before unattended paper deployment.
- Full unattended paper trading requires local market data, a functioning LEAN runtime, Alpaca paper credentials, network access, and an observed session. No profitable performance claim is made.
