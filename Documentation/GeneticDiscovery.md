# Genetic strategy discovery for LEAN

This branch adds an independent, deterministic genetic search module and connects it to LEAN's existing optimizer and backtest runner. The Algory investigation remains behavioral research only; no Algory executable, service, protected code, or license mechanism is used here.

The repository baseline is upstream LEAN commit `22404ea28b8d3f3c9eb74d6ed9f0444426f8e417`, verified as upstream `master` on 2026-10-28. GitHub's latest non-prerelease tag is `v2.4.0.1` from 2017, so the fork uses the current upstream source head instead of that old release tag. The full source SHA plus the SDK pin makes the build baseline reproducible.

## Architecture

- `StrategyDiscovery/QuantConnect.Genetic.csproj` is the standalone engine. A genome selects long-only trend-following, mean-reversion, or breakout logic, a primary minute/hour/daily resolution with an optional slower timeframe confirmation, moving-average and volatility periods, entry/exit thresholds, position count, and gross exposure. The breakout family enters above a shorter high channel and exits below a longer low channel, both computed from completed prior bars. Higher-timeframe filters are built from LEAN consolidators over the primary subscription, and an active filter must be ready and directionally agree before the primary signal can create a portfolio target.
- `Optimizer/Strategies/GeneticSearchOptimizationStrategy.cs` implements LEAN's `IOptimizationStrategy`. It submits candidate parameter sets to LEAN; `ConsoleLeanOptimizer` starts independent LEAN processes and bounds concurrency. Growth and Consistency populations evolve separately. Random candidates provide a baseline.
- Universe selection defaults to the fixed `discovery.symbols` list. Optional `discovery.universe-mode=TopDollarVolume` selects up to `discovery.universe-size` US equity securities daily from LEAN point-in-time coarse data, using a $5 minimum price and $25 million minimum dollar volume. Configured symbols remain as anchors and may include ETFs. Newly added securities receive indicator and return-history warmup; removed securities are liquidated before active strategy state is dropped. Optimizer and paper universe modes and sizes must match, and the supervisor checks this before starting.
- `Algorithm.CSharp/GeneticDiscoveryAlgorithm.cs` evaluates one genome using LEAN securities, indicators, portfolio targets, brokerage fees, and slippage. U.S. equity orders use `AlpacaEquityRegulatoryFeeModel` for estimated CAT fees on buys and sells plus SEC and FINRA TAF fees on sells. Its live-mode guard requires explicit strategy opt-in, `alpaca-paper-trading=true`, and the `AlpacaBrokerage` adapter; other live brokerage adapters are rejected. It budgets remaining position slots and gross exposure after existing holdings outside the managed symbol set, and emits zero targets only for its managed symbols when signals disappear or shutdown is requested. Portfolio correlation uses exact shared return intervals; a candidate is excluded while a pair has fewer than 20 aligned samples. Intraday equity target changes are deferred until a regular-session bar so LEAN does not turn an after-hours market order into a brokerage-invalid market-on-open order. `StrategyDiscovery/PortfolioDrawdownGuard.cs` persists the account high watermark and a latched drawdown trip across LEAN process restarts; the paper supervisor stops optimization/restarts after a trip. A file-based emergency stop is checked on data events. Brokerage disconnects pause new target submissions and liquidation orders until reconnection; an invalid order latches the execution safety gate, with liquidation deferred to the next data event to avoid reentrant order submission, and stops the algorithm when flat. Simulated market orders add a configurable half-spread floor (default 5 basis points full spread) plus LEAN's volume-share impact model; the spread floor is an assumption, not quote-derived execution data. The equity regulatory fee estimate uses Alpaca's fee schedule revised 2026-09-17.
- Training generations are scored on the configured training dates. Finalists from both evolved populations and random candidates are rerun across contiguous chronological windows in the disjoint validation date interval. Evolved finalists also get deterministic one-axis parameter probes around their fast/slow periods, entry/exit thresholds, and available slower confirmation timeframes. A finalist must finish every central window, satisfy constraints in every central window, and have valid results for every requested probe. Its score is the lower of central lower-median fitness and neighbor lower-median fitness. After all validation candidates have been compared, exactly one highest-scoring eligible evolved candidate is run once on the later, disjoint confirmation interval. This final period is not used to choose among candidates; it is the sole basis for optimizer `Solution` and a required gate for paper eligibility. JSONL evaluation history and ranked training/validation/confirmation records are written under LEAN's results folder. The ranking file compares evolved finalists with the median held-out random baseline and records probe count, valid probe count, weakest neighbor fitness, and fitness delta. When LEAN provides closed trades, history includes both a seeded IID bootstrap and seeded without-replacement trade-order permutations, with 1,000 permutations by default, recording 95th-percentile and worst drawdown plus the fraction above the 40% diagnostic threshold.
- The example algorithm runs scheduled health reviews every configured number of calendar days, emits a reoptimization request in its log, and retires after repeated negative review periods or a configured drawdown. The paper supervisor consumes reoptimization requests, validates a newly selected genome against the configured promotion thresholds, and restarts only an approved paper session.
- Alpaca is a separate official LEAN brokerage plugin. Run `pwsh ./scripts/setup-alpaca-plugin.ps1` before enabling paper deployment. The installer checks out plugin commit `b92d6ecd391d5a79e91b060668d1ea4fafec9b89`, pins its LEAN package references to `2.5.18208`, and builds the adapter into `plugins/alpaca`. Configure `alpaca-plugin-directory` in `examples/genetic-discovery/supervisor.json` to that directory. The supervisor refuses to start without the adapter and `Alpaca.Markets.dll`, and passes the validated folder to LEAN's `plugin-directory` loader. The staged directory preserves the upstream plugin README and includes Apache-2.0 license notices for both the QuantConnect plugin and its Alpaca SDK dependency. Focused CI builds the plugin and runs its offline factory/composition test; broker connection and account tests require Alpaca credentials and are not run here. No broker session is claimed without Alpaca paper credentials.
- CI NuGet audit reports advisories in inherited LEAN/plugin dependencies, including `System.Drawing.Common` 4.7.0 (NU1904), `DotNetZip` 1.16.0 (NU1903), and `MessagePack` 3.1.7 (NU1902). These advisories have not been triaged or upgraded in this branch; review the dependency tree before any production deployment.

The engine's seeded generator makes candidate creation and evolution reproducible. Evaluation order does not affect the next generation because breeding begins only after every candidate in that generation has completed. LEAN's optimizer controls process concurrency. Randomized-baseline comparisons report the empirical tail probability and its Bonferroni adjustment across evolved validation finalists. This controls finalist comparison multiplicity only; it does not correct the training-stage search or the fact that randomized baselines are selected by training fitness. Treat the values as diagnostics, not confirmatory evidence. The default example validates two random baselines, too few to resolve a 5% tail threshold; increase `validation-baseline-count` and `random-baseline-size` for finer resolution. Paper promotion requires an evolved candidate to beat the validation baseline median, pass walk-forward and parameter robustness gates, and have a passing result in the untouched confirmation interval. The single confirmation result is not a statistical comparison against the randomized baseline.

## Build and run a local discovery search

The SDK is pinned in `global.json` to .NET SDK 10.0.403. Build from the repository root, then launch the optimizer with the checked-in config:

```powershell
dotnet build QuantConnect.Lean.sln
Push-Location Optimizer.Launcher/bin/Debug
dotnet QuantConnect.Optimizer.Launcher.dll --config ../../../examples/genetic-discovery/optimizer.json
Pop-Location
```

`optimizer.json` is a modest search example with three chronological validation windows and four parameter probes per evolved finalist. After walk-forward selection, the optimizer runs exactly one final confirmation backtest on the selected evolved genome; only that result can become `Solution` or qualify for paper promotion. The confirmation range must be later than and disjoint from both training and validation. `optimizer-smoke.json` runs eleven LEAN backtests against the March 2021 SPY data archive, with one validation window, one probe per evolved finalist, and a final untouched March 15–31 confirmation window. Ensure LEAN's matching US equity data is installed before running. Result and LEAN launcher paths are relative to `Optimizer.Launcher/bin/Debug`.

The genetic optimizer is self-starting: it generates its own genome parameter sets and does not require an input `parameters` range. LEAN's other optimization strategies retain the existing non-empty parameter requirement.

To use point-in-time coarse-universe selection, set `universe-mode` to `TopDollarVolume` and `universe-size` to the same value in optimizer settings and paper overlay parameters (`discovery.universe-mode` and `discovery.universe-size`). The checked-in examples default to `Configured`. The local integration fixture uses the 2014 coarse archive; local coarse coverage is sparse, and availability of coarse selection in an Alpaca live-data session has not been verified.

## Look-ahead prefix-invariance check

Run `examples/genetic-discovery/verify-prefix-invariance.ps1` from PowerShell after building the launcher and algorithm. It runs the same fixed SPY genome twice over the identical 2019-01-01-2021-02-15 interval, then compares fills through 2021-02-10 for repeatability. It also compares that prefix against a longer run ending 2021-03-31 and validates each run's persisted drawdown state. The script requires at least one fill and saves complete runs and launcher logs under `%TEMP%\lean-prefix-invariance` by default. The latest run completed all three prefix checks: 146 daily fills, 67 hourly/daily-confirmation fills, and 355 multi-asset fills through the cutoff matched their longer-history runs. The multi-asset test runs SPY, QQQ, and IWM together; LEAN filled all three symbols, and each logged portfolio selection stayed within three positions and 60% gross target exposure. A same-genome LEAN cost-stress comparison on SPY reported 9.939% net profit with a 5 bps full-spread floor and 2.628% with a 200 bps floor. The 5 bps run recorded $7.17 of Alpaca equity regulatory fees. A separate 2019–2020 SPY run tripped the 5% account drawdown guard, persisted the latch, liquidated to flat, and submitted no later strategy orders. This confirms spread, volume-impact slippage, the equity fee estimate, and account drawdown shutdown affect simulated results. This cost sensitivity check does not calibrate the assumption to historical quotes. The dynamic-universe prefix check matched 6 fills through 2014-04-02 between short and longer histories; the full run selected changing securities from dated coarse records, including AAPL, and filled IWM alongside the SPY anchor. These are repeatability and causal prefix checks for this algorithm/data path and bounded portfolio integration checks; they do not prove general absence of leakage.

## Alpaca paper execution

`StrategyDiscovery.Supervisor` runs the optimizer on a schedule, requires a fresh ranking file, approves only evolved candidates that pass per-window trade/drawdown gates and probe checks, and starts LEAN using the promoted genome. The supervisor keeps the current paper process running during optimization. It polls the emergency-stop file once per `emergency-stop-poll-seconds` (default one second), creates that file for promotion or shutdown, and waits up to `paper-shutdown-timeout-seconds` (default 30) for LEAN to close managed positions and exit. If LEAN does not exit in time, the supervisor terminates its process tree, records the timeout, leaves the stop file in place, and does not restart trading. Optimizer failures leave a running paper process untouched. Unexpected paper-process exits use exponential backoff and stop after the configured consecutive-failure limit. Its JSONL audit and child process logs go under the configured output directory.

The supervisor is Alpaca-paper-only. It refuses to start without both the `--enable-paper` command-line switch and `ALGORY_ALPACA_API_KEY` / `ALGORY_ALPACA_API_SECRET` environment variables. It merges `examples/genetic-discovery/paper-config.overlay.json` over LEAN's base config, forces `environment=live-alpaca`, `alpaca-paper-trading=true`, and the algorithm paper gate, and rejects optimizer/paper mismatches in symbols or risk caps. A single-instance file lock prevents two supervisors using the same output directory. The supervisor records the LEAN child PID, start time, ephemeral config path, and promoted genome in `active-paper-session.json`. After a supervisor crash, a new instance verifies that marker against the live-process start time and the still-present paper-only config, then adopts the process instead of starting a duplicate. Paper sessions use LEAN's file log handler and a unique results directory inside supervisor output so logs remain available after the supervisor process exits. Legacy or unverifiable markers fail closed for operator reconciliation. A stale marker clears its private temporary config when the process has exited or its PID was reused. The adopted child remains monitored and is stopped through the ordinary emergency-stop path during reoptimization or supervisor shutdown. The persistent portfolio drawdown state is stored as `portfolio-drawdown-state.json` beside the emergency-stop file; once tripped, automatic optimization and paper restarts stop until an operator reviews and resets that state. The generated config contains credentials, is stored under the current user's temporary directory, uses owner-only Unix permissions, and is deleted when its LEAN child exits. The supervisor has no real-money mode.

To build and run the unattended paper supervisor after setting Alpaca **paper** credentials through a secret manager:

```powershell
dotnet build QuantConnect.Lean.sln
dotnet StrategyDiscovery.Supervisor/bin/Debug/QuantConnect.Genetic.Supervisor.dll --config examples/genetic-discovery/supervisor.json --enable-paper
```

`Ctrl+C` requests shutdown through the emergency-stop file. A manually present emergency-stop file is honored and prevents a restart. Running this workflow has not been demonstrated because this environment has no Alpaca paper credentials. Real-money live trading remains disabled by default and is not supported by this supervisor.

### Local package inspection (REA, static)

On 2026-10-27, REA inspected the installed Algory 1.5.3.1.8 package without executing it. The application has separate `Algory.exe`, `AlgoryHUD.exe`, and `Engine.exe` files. Static archive inspection identified both `Algory.exe` and `Engine.exe` as PyInstaller one-file packages carrying CPython 3.13. `Engine.exe` is the likely strategy-engine host: its archive contains an `Algory_Engine_v129` entry, a `PYZ.pyz/Algory_Genes_v16.pyc` module, and a compiled `MetaTrader5` package. The smaller `Algory.exe` package instead contains an `Algory_Dashboard_v119` entry plus `Algory_Genes_v16.pyc`, `Algory_UI_Lib_v1.pyc`, and `Algory_Vault_v1.pyc`. REA verified the inspected archive entries against their container integrity metadata.

This narrows the engine location to the Python payload hosted by `Engine.exe`, with related strategy code also bundled in the dashboard package. It does not yet prove which bytecode functions perform breeding, fitness scoring, or backtesting, or whether the GUI launches this engine for a particular workflow. No Algory or engine process was running during this inspection; only the HUD server/window processes were present. The currently available static evidence therefore does not establish runtime call paths, cloud responsibilities, paid entitlements, or function-level implementation. The previous controlled runtime observation recorded MT5 initialization attempts and historical-data requests to an Algory service, but those observations do not prove where all strategy calculations or persistence occur. The installed package also contains a `pyarmor_runtime_011532` support directory; protected modules were not decrypted or bypassed. No third-party binary or code is used by this implementation.

The identified engine host and embedded strategy module are stronger evidence than the prior shell-level identification, but exact Algory genome, genetic operators, fitness formulas, and backtest implementation remain unverified. This project reimplements those behaviors independently and does not evade protections.

The observed application boundary is partial: the desktop UI/HUD and protected engine process run locally; the runtime attempted MetaTrader 5 initialization and fetched historical data from an Algory service. Available evidence does not establish which ranking, storage, portfolio, or monitoring features are local versus server-side, nor which exact capabilities are subscription entitlements. No license bypass is attempted.

## Limitations and remaining work

- Optimizer and LEAN paper processes are coordinated by the supervisor. A process identity marker and single-instance lock prevent duplicate paper sessions after a supervisor crash; a marker with valid paper config is adopted, while a legacy or unverifiable marker fails closed. Adoption preserves the surviving LEAN process's in-memory strategy and brokerage connection; it does not reconstruct that state after the LEAN process itself exits. Process-boundary tests verify both adoption of a surviving child and fail-closed behavior for a legacy marker.
- Walk-forward validation partitions the holdout into contiguous calendar windows and scores the lower median fitness. It does not add purging/embargo gaps or carry open positions between independent LEAN backtests. Parameter probes alter one genome value at a time by fixed increments; this local sensitivity check does not establish broad parameter stability. Monte Carlo diagnostics use closed-trade P&L divided by entry notional, with IID bootstrap resampling and without-replacement permutations of observed trade order. The shuffle report records, but does not gate on, the fraction of permutations above 40% maximum drawdown; the public Algory page does not specify enough detail to calibrate an equivalent rejection rule. The held-out randomized-strategy comparison reports an empirical upper-tail probability with a plus-one correction and Bonferroni adjustment across evolved finalists; because both evolved candidates and randomized baselines are selected using training fitness, this is a diagnostic rather than a calibrated significance test.
- Multi-timeframe confirmation is a slower directional/regime filter using the same periods and thresholds as the primary signal; it does not evolve independent parameters per timeframe or support a faster confirmation series. The local integration check exercised hourly SPY bars with daily confirmation indicators through readiness and checked that no invalid order tripped the safety gate.
- `TopDollarVolume` uses LEAN's dated coarse records rather than a present-day survivor list, but ranks by previous-day price and dollar volume only; no fundamental quality filters are applied. Local coarse records cover only a sparse 2014 sample, and live coarse selection availability with Alpaca has not been verified. The available SPY daily archive ends on 2021-03-31, so other smoke runs cannot test current market regimes or long-span corporate-action behavior. Alpaca regulatory rates are fixed to its 2026-09-17 schedule for every historical date; LEAN charges them per order without Alpaca's daily account-level per-fee rounding, and this model uses the NMS per-share CAT rate rather than the OTC executed-share adjustment, so this is an approximate cost model, not a historical fee ledger. The integration smoke script runs AAPL across its August 2020 split and dividend in adjusted mode, then runs a raw-mode buy-and-hold check that asserts one share becomes four and a positive dividend is credited to the held position. Cash-in-lieu handling and broad corporate-action coverage remain unverified.
- Account slot/exposure calculations include outside-universe brokerage holdings, but cannot attribute positions in the configured symbols to other algorithms or manual orders. Concurrent algorithms can race these checks, so use a dedicated paper account until broker-level coordination is implemented and tested.
- LEAN Engine's brokerage message router is exercised with emitted `BacktestingBrokerage` disconnect/reconnect events, including the strategy order gate. Rejected-order shutdown is also exercised through the in-process `BacktestingBrokerage` → `BrokerageTransactionHandler` → algorithm callback path; this does not substitute for observing a real Alpaca rejection. The supervisor process test checks prompt emergency-stop polling and force termination after the configured shutdown timeout when a paper child ignores the stop file. Recovery tests cover a surviving child across launcher exit, but do not crash an actual `SupervisorHost` while it manages a LEAN process or restore brokerage state. Paper-session order outcomes and concurrent broker-level risk coordination still need integration tests before unattended paper deployment.
- A forced process shutdown after the configured timeout stops autonomous trading but cannot guarantee positions were liquidated first. Inspect broker positions and audit logs before clearing the emergency-stop file. A fixed historical confirmation interval is reused by scheduled optimizer runs; repeated exposure to its results can erode its independence, so roll the configured dates forward for ongoing research. Full unattended paper trading requires local market data, a functioning LEAN runtime, Alpaca paper credentials, network access, and an observed session. No profitable performance claim is made.
